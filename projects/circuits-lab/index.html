Circuits Lab

A browser and VR bench for wiring circuits, using instruments, and exploring four Electrical Circuits I experiments.

Use the circuit bench

Connect components, change values, and read the meters and graphs.

Open Circuits Lab full screen →

For VR, open the lab full screen in a compatible headset browser and select Enter VR.

I built Circuits Lab for four Electrical Circuits I experiments: Thévenin and Norton equivalents with maximum power transfer, superposition, operational amplifiers, and RC/RL transient response.

Explore starts with a connected circuit. Build circuit gives you components and contacts to wire yourself. Pick up a meter probe and place its tip on a contact, pull out a plug to change a connection, turn a dial, or flip a switch. Students write their readings, calculations, and answers on paper; the app is for doing the experiments.

The load-power sweep and signed source contributions are calculated from the actual connections. The scope reads the selected circuit nodes. Transient graphs grow as the circuit runs; dragging through an acquired trace lets you inspect voltage, current and stored energy at the same instant. Undo restores a probe or lead placement.

In VR, grip probes and plugs, hold and turn dials, and use the left stick to move and the right stick to turn. Recenter brings you back to the bench. The experiment controls sit close by on a front-right ledge, with large button labels. Graphs stay on the left device. The right panel shows readings; the centre panel explains circuit issues and offers the circuit diagram. Reset lab restores the original Explore circuit, values, probes and instrument settings. The amplifier has more space around its resistors, separate sockets along its common ground, and clearer wire routes. Probes point down when picked up and then follow your wrist. Moving a lead keeps other wires in place, and cables settle gently after release. Large readings sit beside the equipment; hover text no longer covers the bench. This is a teaching prototype with ideal circuit models. The amplifier configurations are provisional because the lab handout has not been provided. Physical headset testing remains outstanding for the revised panels and reset control in v0.10.

## Active architecture

`main.js` routes Lab 7 to `amplifier-app.js`. `amplifier-experiment.js` owns one electrical state, manual actions, undo snapshots, observer hints and acquisition. `amplifier-display.js` shares scope scale formatting and the phone dual-channel plot. `amplifier-scene.js` builds the board, terminals, variable-resistor knobs, raised instrument cases and functional generator controls. Neighboring labs retain `legacy-main.js` and their established model/bench helpers.

The experiment starts with the supply off and no leads or probes attached. A student drags between terminals, or taps two sockets and presses the contextual Connect button. The named From/To and instrument forms are retained inside the default-closed, keyboard-accessible Connection tools disclosure. Incorrect connections persist and are diagnosed. Completing the circuit leaves disconnected instruments missing. Hints and Another hint never modify electrical state. Reset and configuration switches are single undoable actions with no hidden saved wiring modes.

AC amplitude is Vpp; DC is signed volts. All physical resistor knobs and numeric fields share exact, independent values. Inverting/non-inverting also provide sliders. The adder exposes Rin1/Rin2/Rf; the difference amplifier exposes R1/R2/R3/R4 and an explicit, one-step-undoable Match R4 action. Two source amplitudes are independent while waveform, frequency and phase are shared explicitly. Single-input circuits report measured gain; two-input circuits show weighted transfer coefficients, including the general unmatched difference formula. The meter measures its current probes independently of scope Hold. Each valid scope channel has its own statistics; paired samples are required only for measured gain. Both traces share one time grid and voltage scale. Wrong scope grounds suppress their channel and produce an explicit diagnostic. Channel captions identify the actual probe target, including on a held capture. Passive resistors accept either terminal orientation; the original lead endpoints and measured socket voltages are retained.

The DIP-8 has the actual top-view pin order. Pins 1 and 5 are offset-null and pin 8 is NC; unused pins are excluded from the exercise. Mounting holes are isolated. Several physically distributed GND sockets explicitly share the labeled common rail so ground connections can remain local.

## Remaining limitations

The dedicated Lab 7 app targets desktop and phone presentation. It has no XR entry, session or controller path; immersive support is not implemented here. Physical touch hardware and headset operation were not tested. Desktop pointer and responsive-browser checks cannot establish those hardware behaviors. Source terminal pairs can be measured before the amplifier is complete. Other incomplete or incorrect amplifier networks produce no simulated amplifier readings: this is a bounded teaching topology model, not a general arbitrary-network breadboard solver. The model is an ideal 741 approximation with 1 V educational output headroom; bandwidth, slew rate, loading, offsets and input common-mode limits are omitted.

Full Jekyll/site validation remains blocked by system Ruby 2.6.10 and missing lockfile-required Bundler 2.7.2. Standalone Vite builds and Node tests are separate checks. The local Vite bundle is rebuilt for each submitted review checkpoint. Generated files are not edited manually, and a successful build is not visual acceptance.

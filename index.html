working profile

I turn uncertain technical work into decision systems people can operate.

My work sits where models, labs, and policy questions have to become something reliable enough for other people to use. The model matters, but the larger job is making the assumptions visible, the workflow repeatable, and the output inspectable before a decision hardens.

That usually means moving between technical detail and operating context: translating stakeholder questions, building simulation or optimization prototypes, documenting the logic, and leaving behind systems that can survive handoff.

how the work moves

From ambiguous question to usable system

I am most useful in the middle of the work: where the brief is still messy, the model is not enough by itself, and the final artifact has to make sense to people outside the build process.

  1. 01
    Frame the decision

    Clarify the real question, constraints, audience, and what a useful answer needs to change.

  2. 02
    Make the logic inspectable

    Build models, scenarios, experiments, and assumptions that can be reviewed instead of guessed at.

  3. 03
    Turn it into a workflow

    Package the work with documentation, interfaces, routines, and enough structure for handoff.

  4. 04
    Translate across the room

    Keep technical systems legible to operators, researchers, vendors, and policy stakeholders.

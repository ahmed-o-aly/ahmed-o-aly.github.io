What I am building this site for

Created on May 15, 2026

2026   ·   simulation   public-policy   optimization   books   ·   blog

I want this site to be more than a static CV. The work I do sits between research, software, operations, and institutions, so it needs a place where the practical details can live.

The professional side will cover the KU MetaHub, ADSG public-policy simulation work, optimization, energy systems, and research software. The writing side will be looser: notes from papers and books, small modeling ideas, lessons from building research software, and reflections on facilities or lab systems when they teach something useful.

Some posts will be technical. Some will be short reading notes. Some will be a sketch of a simulation idea before it becomes a polished project. That mix feels right for the kind of work I want to keep doing: rigorous enough to be useful, but alive enough to keep learning in public.

I built UAE Economy Lab to explore how trade, productivity, and demand affect the UAE economy. It uses ADB's input-output accounts for 2024, covering 35 industries and 74 external partner records, with a simplified single-country CGE model.

Combine changes to import costs, mining demand, and productivity, then fine-tune individual sectors. Follow suppliers, customers, and trading partners in the sector workspace, adjust the response parameters, and compare saved scenarios. Results and inputs can be exported as CSV.

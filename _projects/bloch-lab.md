Start with one arrow and two possible measurement results. Tilt the preparation yourself, predict from its angle, then compare the chance bar with measured copies. Short prompts explain the purpose before each action.

The guided path builds toward relative phase, interference (HH versus HZH), and rotation order (HZ versus ZH, measured along X). Every gate retains its animated rotation trail. Optional free controls expose presets, all gates, X/Y/Z measurements, editable sequences and angle/ket details. Measurements use fresh independent preparations; there is no queued intermediate measurement.

The same guided actions, direct preparation, free exploration, reset and recenter are available inside VR. This is an ideal pure-state teaching model; animation durations are illustrative. Physical headset validation remains pending.

Inspect complete devices in the arrangement, then isolate them to see their construction. A six-step guide connects the visible equipment with Dr Tarek El Fouly’s ELEN424 notes and reference video. The circuit breaker includes its three enclosed tanks, angled bushings and common frame; the transformer includes its tank, cooling banks and insulated terminals.

In VR, one grip moves and rotates the model, while two grips rotate and scale it. Left-stick movement and right-stick turning remain available while gripping. A compact panel appears on demand with equipment selection, the guide, source appearance, isolation and view controls. Use **Bring closer** for a device or **Room size** to walk around the arrangement. Physical Quest headset verification remains pending.

The model supports inspection of construction and relationships. Disconnect and surge-arrester candidates remain explicitly provisional, and several column and compact devices have unresolved electrical roles. The source supplies no verified switching diagram, voltage ratings or transformer internals; these are not inferred from appearance.

“Substation” by **One80 Solar**, [original model on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/substation-8f4e54879b664104bece03d8e7236c15), is used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes include OBJ triangulation, source-piece grouping, exact attribute packing, compressed delivery, optional smoothed normals, representative material finishes and teaching annotations. All 665,472 triangles are retained. Source appearance remains available for comparison.

[Open the three-phase transformer](/power-systems-lab/?model=transformer) to inspect the tank, terminals, inner BT and outer AT coils, core limbs and yokes. Open the enclosure, isolate components and use illustrative separation to study placement. The model retains 248 source occurrences and 838,253 placed triangles.

The academic transformer is by **Patrick Kayter** (2013), with drawings credited to **Patrick Kayter and Jonathan Lucas**; [original GrabCAD model](https://grabcad.com/library/transformador-trifasico). It is published with creator permission. Changes include STEP conversion to GLB, source-part grouping, display normalization, representative finishes and teaching annotations. The source drawings establish the coil and core arrangement; winding turns, rated ratio and electrical connections remain unspecified.

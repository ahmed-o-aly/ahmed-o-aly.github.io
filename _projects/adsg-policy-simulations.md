I support the Abu Dhabi School of Government department at Khalifa University. My work sits between running the department's facilities and developing simulation ideas for public-policy questions. Some days that means making sure the rooms, equipment, and workflows are ready. Other days it means turning a proposal or policy problem into something that can be tested.

Policy questions rarely arrive as neat model variables. I work through the people involved, the decision that needs to be made, the assumptions behind it, and the parts that are uncertain. From there I can build a scenario, decide what should change between runs, and show the result in a form that people can question.

I use simulation as a way to slow a decision down before it becomes fixed. A model does not remove uncertainty, but it can make an assumption visible and show where two reasonable choices lead in different directions. The Abu Dhabi Urban Dynamics Lab is one working example of that approach.

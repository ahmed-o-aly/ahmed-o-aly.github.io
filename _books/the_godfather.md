A sprawling crime-family novel about power, loyalty, incentives, and institutions. I am keeping books on the site because reading notes are useful raw material: they sharpen how I think about systems, people, and decisions.

What stayed with me most is how much of the book is about structure rather than spectacle. Reputation, rules, favors, family obligations, and fear all become operating constraints. That is a useful lens even outside fiction: people rarely act inside clean mathematical abstractions, and systems are shaped by informal incentives as much as formal ones.

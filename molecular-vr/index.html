Evidence & limitations

Each endpoint uses deposited Cα coordinates after a rigid fit of the shared 20S core to 7W38. Differences include protein conformation and experimental/model uncertainty.

The state order is inferred by the authors. ED3 was not captured; playback stops at ED2.1. These six states already have open core gates.

The substrate sequence and residue correspondence are unassigned. It is shown only in observed snapshots and hidden during interpolation. Unmatched protein residues are also hidden between snapshots. Missing coordinates do not mean that a domain has dissociated.

The 7W38 density is hidden in this comparison because it belongs to a different experimental state. Displacement statistics use common protein Cα residues after core alignment, not tracked substrate movement.

In a VR headset

Open this viewer’s HTTPS address in a WebXR-compatible headset browser and choose Enter VR. For a tethered headset, use a compatible desktop browser with your headset runtime running.

Hold either lower grip to move and turn the model. Hold both lower grips and move your hands apart or together to scale it like a ball. The left thumbstick moves you forward, backward, and sideways relative to where you are looking, without changing the model’s size. Release the stick to stop. Supported hand tracking uses pinch to grab.

Press X or click the right thumbstick to summon or hide the controls panel. It opens beside the model when you enter VR and stays where you placed it. Aim either controller at a control and use its trigger to select or drag. Adjust contour, opacity, chain isolation, density display, and cutaway in the panel. Choose State comparison there for playback and focus controls; density remains hidden because its map belongs to 7W38.

On Oculus Touch-style controllers, Y recenters the model; A plays or pauses state comparison and B advances a state. The panel also has recenter and previous/next buttons. Other controller layouts may differ. Hand tracking can grab the model; the panel currently requires controllers.

Immersive VR requires headset support and a secure HTTPS address (or localhost on the same device). Desktop 3D works without a headset.
